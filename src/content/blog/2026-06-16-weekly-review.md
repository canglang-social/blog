Last week I caught myself avoiding the work that mattered by staying busy with easier things. The headline casualty was my portfolio project: never started. This week, the very same project moved — I finished about half of RAGX v1. What changed wasn't willpower. It was the environment around the work.

上周，我发现自己靠忙于更容易的事来逃避真正重要的工作。最大的牺牲品是我的作品集项目：从未开始。这周，同一个项目动起来了——我完成了大约一半的 RAGX v1。改变的不是意志力，而是工作周围的环境。

Three things made it almost automatic: a regular sleep routine, going to a café every day, and committing to a single project. The sequence became fixed — wake up, go to the café, open RAGX. There was no menu of "interesting" options to choose from, no decision to agonize over, and so nothing to avoid. The certainty did the work that motivation kept failing to do.

三件事让它几乎变成自动：规律的睡眠、每天去咖啡馆、以及只专注一个项目。流程被固定下来——起床、去咖啡馆、打开 RAGX。没有一堆"有趣"选项的菜单可挑，没有要纠结的决定，于是也没有可逃避的东西。是确定性完成了动机一直没能完成的事。

That reframes last week's lesson. The gym habit stuck because it was low-friction and instantly rewarding; my job-hunt tasks lacked exactly that. A fixed routine plus one project gives the deep work the same low friction — you remove the decision, and removing the decision removes the avoidance. Momentum then compounds day to day instead of resetting each morning.

这重新诠释了上周的功课。健身习惯能坚持，是因为它低阻力且即时回报；而我找工作的任务恰恰缺这个。固定 routine 加上一个项目，让深度工作有了同样的低阻力——你消除决策，而消除决策就消除了逃避。势头于是一天天累积，而不是每天早上重置。

Not everything held. The JD list slipped again, this time because RAGX pulled me in completely — I'm telling myself the artifact comes first, and I'll be watching next week whether that's real strategy or a comfortable delay. Next week is built on what worked: finish RAGX v1, take a second build-from-scratch project to 30%, and finally start the JDs — all on the same wake-café-build rails that carried this week.

并非一切都守住了。JD 清单又被搁置了，这次是因为 RAGX 把我完全吸了进去——我告诉自己作品优先，下周我会留意这究竟是真策略还是舒适的拖延。下周建立在有效的做法之上：完成 RAGX v1、把第二个从零搭建的项目推到 30%、并终于开始列 JD——全都沿着这周撑住我的"起床—咖啡馆—构建"轨道。
