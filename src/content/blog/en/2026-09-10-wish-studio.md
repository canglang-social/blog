I was working on an AI services business, but it felt too formal and aimed at companies. I wanted to try something more playful for individuals.

The idea that came to mind was a wishing tree. What if you could tell me something you wished existed, and we could work out how to make it?

That became Wish Studio, or 脑洞实现局 in Chinese. I asked AI to help build a page on my domain where people could leave an idea and an email address for a reply.

The page offers a few starting points: a birthday game filled with jokes only your friends understand, a comic starring your cat, or a little tool for an annoying everyday task. These are possible creations, not completed customer projects. Your wish can be different.

Getting the invitation to work took another step. When I first clicked “Tell me your wish,” it opened an empty page. I asked for a place to type directly on the site and a way for the message to reach me. The page now has that form; a test submission reached my email.

I shared the site on X and WeChat Moments. It has English and Chinese versions, and the invitation is simple: describe the thing you wish someone could make.

There is still a practical conversation after the wish. I check what is feasible, and we agree on the result, price, timing and revisions before work begins.

For now, [Wish Studio](https://wish.felixhan.dev/en/) is open for those little ideas. What is something you wish existed?
